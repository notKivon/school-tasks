Touch. No hover on phones, so the trash icon stays visible in a quiet gray. Drag starts on a long press (250ms) so the column still scrolls sideways under your thumb.

Tabs. Tap a tab to snap to that column (try it on the left phone). The active tab follows when you swipe. Short tab names are for the strip only; the column header keeps the full name.

Sheets. Classes, Calendar and the account menu open as a bottom sheet with a grabber, over a dimmed board. Swipe down or tap outside to close.

Inputs. Add-task and title inputs use 16px on phones so iOS doesn't zoom.
